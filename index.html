CABLE!!

In lighting, we use cable to send power and data to our lights. Of course, different lights have diffferent requirements for both, so this will be a shallow overview of different types of cables and when you woud need to use them.

Power Cables

Before we start talking about the different types of connectors you might find on a power cable, its important to talk about safety. Each cable should have 3 wires, a hot, neutral, and ground.

Hot is what sends live electricity to the fixture. Neutral takes the spend current and sends it back to the power supply to complete the circut. the ground is incredibly important, because if either of the other cables fail for some reason, the ground will divert the current. if not for the ground wire, the entire cable might become electrified and incredibly dangerous.

DO NOT PLUG SOMETHING IN IF THE GROUND IS BROKEN

Now that that is out of the way, we can discuss the different connectors you might see on a power cable

Stagepin This is the connector type that is most commonly used in older theaters with incandescent fixtures (we will probably discuss the difference between incandescent and LED fixtures later). The power infrastructure in both LaGuardia theaters runs on stage pin cable.

Edison Most household appliances use edison connectors, but there has been a recent uptick in the amount of lighting equipment that can be adapted to edison. This is because of the popularity of using exisiting edison outlets to power LED fixtures and then just running dmx through access points instead of hanging trusses.
